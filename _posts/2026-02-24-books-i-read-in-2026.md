<p>
These are the books that I've read so far in 2026. And by "read", I also include audio books that I've listened to.
</p>

<ul>
<li>The Power of Habit by Charles Duhigg<br>
Pretty useful, and explains a lot about why people do the things they do.
</li>
<li>On The Road by Jack Kerouac<br>
The first of my "Beat Generation" experiences.
</li>
<li>Naked Lunch by William S. Burroughs<br>
I'm glad I didn't read this Beat Generation book first. Much more interesting the farther I got into it.
</li>
<li>Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep? by Philip K. Dick<br>
If you've seen "Blade Runner", that's not this. Some of the ideas are the same, but it is not the "movie version" of this book. The book is better, and highly influential, but, honestly, not as astounding as I was thinking it would be.
</li>
</ul>
